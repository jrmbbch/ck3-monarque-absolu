«L'État, c'est moi.» Monarque Absolu gives you the tools of an all-powerful sovereign: legendary traits, title seizing, instant construction, a perfect bloodline and more, through character interactions and decisions.

Built for Crusader Kings III 1.20 and every DLC up to By God Alone: feudal, administrative, nomadic, landless, Japanese and Chinese realms are all covered.

Traits

Named after French legends and historical figures, in every language:
• Mélusine, the undying fairy of legend: never looks older than 25, no natural death.
• Louis XIV, the Sun King: 100 in every skill, huge income and every DLC resource, health, fertility, scheme bonuses and protection, much higher domain and vassal limits under any government.
• Colbert, his tireless minister: near-instant and free construction in every currency, domiciles included, county control and development, bigger vassal contributions.
• Napoléon: battle advantage, lightning sieges and marches, endless supply, stronger men-at-arms and knights, raids and winter warfare.

Character interactions

• Grant or revoke each trait, or all powers at once.
• Perfect bloodline: Beautiful, Genius, Divine and Fecund with no genetic flaw, passed on to every new generation.
• Perfect education: raises the education trait to the top of its track.
• All perks: unlocks every perk of every lifestyle at once.
• Seize a title (with or without its vassals, landless titles included) or a whole domain or realm.

Decisions

• Fill the coffers: gold, piety, prestige, renown and every other resource your government uses.
• One realm, one culture and One realm, one faith.
• Mend body and realm: cures every disease across the realm and the wounds of your closest kin.
• Purify the bloodline of your closest kin (vassals keep their own flaws).
• Fortify and arm the domain: founds temples and cities in empty baronies, then fills every building slot of your domain with the best buildings the game allows (fortification first, then levies, then gold) and upgrades everything. No extra slot is ever added.
• Dream home: fills every slot of your domicile and upgrades everything, military buildings first.

Compatibility

• CK3 1.20.*, all DLCs supported, none required.
• Does not overwrite any base game file.
• Its interaction menu reuses a base game slot instead of adding a new category, so it cannot clash with other mods.

Languages

Names stay in French everywhere; everything else is translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
