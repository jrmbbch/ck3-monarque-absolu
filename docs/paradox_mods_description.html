«L'État, c'est moi.» Monarque Absolu gives you the tools of an all-powerful sovereign: legendary traits, title seizing, instant construction, a perfect bloodline and more, through character interactions and decisions.

Built for Crusader Kings III 1.20 and every DLC up to By God Alone: feudal, administrative, nomadic, landless, Japanese and Chinese realms are all covered.

Traits

Named after French legends and historical figures, in every language:

  • Mélusine, the undying fairy of legend: never looks older than 25, no natural death.
  • Louis XIV, the Sun King: 100 in every skill, huge income and every DLC resource, health, fertility, scheme bonuses and protection, much higher domain and vassal limits under any government.
  • Colbert, his tireless minister: near-instant and free construction in every currency, domiciles included, county control and development, bigger vassal contributions.
  • Napoléon: battle advantage, lightning sieges and marches, endless supply, stronger men-at-arms and knights, raids and winter warfare.

Character interactions

  • Grant or revoke each trait, or all powers at once.
  • Perfect bloodline: Beautiful, Genius, Divine and Fecund with no genetic flaw, passed on to every new generation.
  • Perfect education: raises the education trait to the top of its track.
  • All perks: unlocks every perk of every lifestyle at once.
  • Seize a title, landless titles included, alone or with its vassals; or seize all their titles: their domain (their vassals swear fealty to you) or their whole realm (their vassals lose their counties and duchies).
  • Claim all their lands: gain pressed claims on their personal titles or their entire realm, including all their vassals' titles, without transferring titles or vassals. Baronies, noble family titles, landless titles and titles you already hold are excluded.
  • Whole realm is selected by default in both Seize all their titles and Claim all their lands; their personal domain remains available as an alternative.

Decisions

  • Fill the coffers: gold, piety, prestige, renown and every other resource your government uses.
  • One realm, one culture and One realm, one faith.
  • Gather the realm: takes back, without war, every county, duchy and kingdom that belongs de jure to your duchies, kingdoms and empires but is held by a foreign ruler.
  • An exceptional council: each councillor gets +100 in the skill of their position (administrative ministers included), for as long as they hold it.
  • Mend body and realm: cures every disease across the realm and the wounds of your closest kin.
  • Purify the bloodline of your closest kin (vassals keep their own flaws).
  • Fortify and arm the domain: fills and upgrades buildings in existing holdings of your counties, including vassal barons, and your baronies held elsewhere. Fortifications first, then levies, then income. Lower-priority buildings may be replaced. This decision does not found, convert or grant holdings.
  • Found new holdings: fills empty secondary baronies in your counties with the missing government-appropriate temple (temple-citadel for mandala), the missing city, then castles, subject to the game's requirements. Tribal and nomadic rulers are excluded. The game may assign some new holders automatically.
  • Convert holdings to castles: converts your eligible controlled tribal, nomadic and herder holdings, including county capitals, when you are neither tribal nor of the steppe. Improvements and grants are separate choices.
  • Grant secondary baronies: grants eligible old and new personal secondary cities, temples, castles and temple-citadels to generated nobles of your culture, faith and rite as direct vassals. County capitals, leased titles and clergy temples are protected; other barons keep their titles.
  • Dream home: fills every slot of your domicile and upgrades everything, military buildings first. A construction underway in the domicile is cancelled and refunded first.
  • Construction and improvements skip costs and construction times while respecting normal building requirements and slot limits. All four domain actions leave active construction untouched. If using all four, the suggested order is conversion, foundation, improvements, then grants. No extra slots are added.

Compatibility

  • CK3 1.20.*, all DLCs supported, none required.
  • Does not overwrite any base game file.
  • Its interaction menu reuses a base game slot instead of adding a new category, so it cannot clash with other mods.

Languages

Names stay in French everywhere; everything else is translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
